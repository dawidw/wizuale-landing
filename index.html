Two apps. Wizuale plays real Milkdrop presets, full screen, on Apple TV. Wizuale Mic is separate, on your iPhone. Same Wi-Fi, and the picture follows the sound in the room.

Two separate apps

One lives on the television. The other lives on the phone. They meet only on your Wi-Fi.

Apple TV

Wizuale

Put on a record, or play music on the TV. The preset fills the screen. Move to another one with the remote, or let the next one come on by itself.

iPhone

Wizuale Mic

Connected to Apple TV

A second app. On the same Wi-Fi it finds the television and shows that connection on the phone. It sends the sound in the room, so the visuals react to what you hear. Pick a preset there, or leave them to advance.

Full screen

The visual is the television. Once it is playing, that is the whole picture.

The sound in the room

Connect the iPhone app. From then on, the picture reacts to what the phone hears.

Pick a preset

Choose one on the phone and the TV stays with it. Or step through them from the remote.

Auto-advance

Leave it on and the preset changes by itself. Pick one when you want the picture to stay.
